3C remote sensing reflectance model, JAX implementation
Author of the JAX implementation: Mortimer Werther (Eawag)
Original 3C model and Python implementation: Philipp Groetsch
Upstream repository: https://gitlab.com/pgroetsch/rrs_model_3C
JAX version: https://gitlab.com/pgroetsch/rrs_model_3C/-/tree/werther_jax_version
Licence of the original implementation: LGPL-3.0-or-later. The copy in this repository is
distributed under GPL-3.0-or-later (full text in LICENSE at the root of this repository).

Reference papers:
Groetsch, P. M. M., Gege, P., Simis, S. G. H., Eleveld, M. A., and Peters, S. W. M. Validation
of a spectral correction procedure for sun and sky reflections in above-water reflectance
measurements. Optics Express 25(16), A742–A761 (2017). https://doi.org/10.1364/OE.25.00A742

Files
  model.py        3C model, cost, jax.grad Jacobian and lmfit L-BFGS-B fit of the paper processing
  adapter.py      interface of this repository (parameters, weights and inputs per measurement)
  spectra/        phyto.A, water.A and Y.A of the 3C repository, daWdT.txt (temperature gradient
                  of pure water absorption, Roettgers et al., Algorithm Theoretical Basis
                  Document of the Water Optical Properties Processor (WOPP), revision 7, 2013)

Changes against the code of the paper processing (Mortimer Werther, 2026-10-28)
  model.py: plotting and the timing printout are removed. fit_LuEd_compiled compiles the cost and
  the gradient once per model. fit_LuEd_compiled gives bitwise the results of fit_LuEd.
