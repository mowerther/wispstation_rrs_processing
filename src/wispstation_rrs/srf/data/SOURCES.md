# Sources of the spectral response functions

**Sentinel-2 MSI (msi_s2a.csv, msi_s2b.csv, msi_s2c.csv, msi_bands.csv).** ESA technical note COPE-GSEG-EOPG-TN-15-0007, Sentinel-2 Spectral Response Functions (S2-SRF), version 4.0, 2024-06-04, distributed as the workbook "COPE-GSEG-EOPG-TN-15-0007 - Sentinel-2 Spectral Response Functions 2024 - 4.0.xlsx". The response tables are the sheets "Spectral Responses (S2A)", "(S2B)" and "(S2C)" (columns SR_WL and S2x_SR_AV_B1 to S2x_SR_AV_B12 with B8A), 300–2600 nm at 1 nm, values unchanged. In msi_bands.csv, centre_nm is the equivalent wavelength (first moment of the response, sheet "Equivalent Wavelengths"), nominal_centre_nm the band centre of the sheet "Overview", and mid_bandwidth_nm and bandwidth_nm come from the sheet "Bandwidth and mid-wavelength".

**Sentinel-3 OLCI (olci_s3a.csv, olci_s3b.csv, olci_bands.csv).** Platform-mean spectral response functions of the OLCI instrument characterisation, files S3A_OL_SRF_20160713_mean_rsr.nc4 (spectral model OLCI_S3A_spectral_model_v7.02_fltrd_20160713, created 2016-07-13) and S3B_OL_SRF_0_20180109_mean_rsr.nc4 (spectral model OLCI_S3B_spectral_model_V1.01_fltrd_20180109, created 2018-01-09), as published by ESA and EUMETSAT. The tables hold the variables mean_spectral_response_function and mean_spectral_response_function_wavelength for the 21 bands, 200 samples per band on each band's own wavelength grid. The values are written as the shortest decimals of the stored single-precision numbers. In olci_bands.csv, centre_nm is srf_centre_wavelength (first moment of the response), nominal_centre_nm is nominal_centre_wavelength and fwhm_nm is bandwidth_fwhm. No Sentinel-3C table is included.

manifest.json lists for each table the source file, release, date and the SHA-256 checksums of the source file and of the table. Copernicus Sentinel data are provided under the free, full and open Copernicus data policy.
